Welcome to Our Simulator Room

Immersive experience with hands-on aviation training using our advanced DGEN 380 Engine Simulator and integrated flight and instrument simulation systems. The Simulator Room enables students to apply classroom knowledge in a controlled environment, understand engine systems, practice instrument interpretation and perform flight operations using simulation platforms..

A Hub for Aspiring Aviators

Our mission is to support the next generation of aviation leaders by providing opportunities for academic growth and collaborative development. Through online flight sessions, aviation discussions, and events, we encourage a community where students can connect, learn and actively participate in shaping their aviation journey and contribute to the development of aviation at Balamand.

Engine Simulator

Explore the operational principles of a modern turbofan engine through interactive hands-on simulation of the DGEN 380 engine simulator, focusing on engine performance, and system behavior

Flight Simulator

Engage with aircraft operations and system through X-Plane simulation, performing online flights, executing procedures, and implementing modifications to virtual aircraft within the simulator environment.

Six Packs

Develop a foundational understanding of aircraft basic flight instruments with emphasis on accurate parameter interpretation, system interconnection, and instrument installation, while examining the operational principles that determine how each instrument functions and displays its indications.

History

The Simulator Room was established in May 2025 to support and inspire students at the University of Balamand. The facility provides a structured environment where students can expand their knowledge of aviation through hands-on simulation using simulation platforms. The development of the Simulator Room reflects a commitment to academic excellence, and aviation enthusiasm at Balamand. It continues to evolve enabling students to translate classroom theory into practical hands-on simulation.
